## Pass 1 — findings: 2

1. `failure-mode-visibility` — **The design did not say that AgentSmith's own pillar nodes would
   leave.** `.cursorrules` is generated and gitignored here (`.gitignore:19`), so the 18 pillar nodes
   built from the owner's local copy go with this change. CI's graph never had them. **Fix:** the
   design and the CHANGELOG say so, and why that is the right reading: the pillars live in the tracked
   `templates/agent-rules.yaml`, and a tenant that commits `.cursorrules` keeps its nodes.
2. `docs-match-behaviour` — **`_kg_shape`'s comment described both defects as current**, as the reason
   Guardrails and untracked files stay out of the comparison. **Fix:** it says they are fixed at the
   mapper. The exclusions stay, for the reason still true: untracked-but-unignored files, a design in
   progress among them, differ between a working tree and a fresh runner.
